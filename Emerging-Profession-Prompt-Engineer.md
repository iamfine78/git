# PART B — Six Individual Speaking Scripts
# 第二部分 —— 六人专属脱稿演说手稿

> **How to use 使用说明：**
> Each script is about **450–520 English words** — roughly **4 to 5 minutes** at a calm speaking speed.
> 每篇约 **450–520 个英文单词**，正常语速约 **4–5 分钟**。
>
> **Golden rule 黄金法则：** the script **adds** to the slide, it never **reads** the slide. When you say a number that is on the screen, point at it and then explain it in your own words.
> 手稿是给 PPT **补充**内容，绝不照读。当你说到屏幕上的数字时，用手指一下，再用自己的话解释。

## Script 1 — Member 1: Job Origin 职业诞生背景

**Speaker 组员 1｜Time 时长: 4–5 min｜PPT: Slides 1, 2, 3**

---

**Opening 开场**

Good morning, everyone. Thank you for coming.
大家早上好，感谢各位到场。

I am from the Career Development Department of the Student Union.
我来自学生会职业发展部。

Today, our group will talk about a new job. Its name is AI Prompt Engineer.
今天，我们小组要讲一个新兴职业，它的名字叫 AI 提示词工程师。

Please do not worry about the word "engineer". You do not need to be good at math today.
请不要被"工程师"这个词吓到，今天你不需要擅长数学。

If you have ever talked to ChatGPT, you are already close to this job.
如果你跟 ChatGPT 聊过天，你其实已经离这个职业很近了。

**The problem 问题在哪里**

Let me start with a simple question. How many of you have used ChatGPT or another AI tool?
我先问一个简单的问题：在座有多少人用过 ChatGPT 或其他 AI 工具？

Okay, most of you. Now, a second question. Did you always get a good answer?
好，大部分人。第二个问题：你每次都能得到好答案吗？

Me neither. Sometimes the answer is great. Sometimes it is totally wrong.
我也不能。有时候答案很棒，有时候完全不对。

Here is the reason. AI is very smart, but it is not a mind reader.
原因在这里：AI 很聪明，但它不会读心。

It does not know what we really want. It only knows what we actually say.
它不知道我们真正想要什么，它只知道我们实际说了什么。

**The gap 缺口**

So there is a gap between us and the AI.
所以在人和 AI 之间出现了一个缺口。

The gap looks like this. A bad question gives a bad answer. A good question gives a good answer.
这个缺口是这样的：烂问题给出烂答案，好问题给出好答案。

The word for "the question we give AI" is **prompt**.
"我们给 AI 的问题"这个词，英文叫 **prompt**。

A prompt can be one short sentence, or it can be two pages long. It depends on the task.
一个 prompt 可以是一句短语，也可以长达两页，取决于任务。

And the person whose job is to write these prompts well — that person is a prompt engineer.
而专门把 prompt 写好的人，就是这个职业：提示词工程师。

**Why it appeared in 2022 为什么是 2022 年**

Why did this job appear so suddenly? Two reasons.
为什么这个职业出现得这么突然？有两个原因。

First, the technology became good enough. In late 2022, ChatGPT came out.
第一，技术够好了。2022 年底，ChatGPT 上线。

For the first time, ordinary people — not just programmers — could use a powerful AI.
普通人——不只是程序员——第一次能用上强大的 AI。

Second, people found that prompts really matter. The same AI could give a boring answer or an amazing answer.
第二，人们发现 prompt 真的很重要。同一个 AI，可以给你无聊的答案，也可以给你惊艳的答案。

The difference was not the AI. The difference was the question.
差别不在 AI，差别在提问。

So a new need appeared, and a new job was born.
于是新需求出现了，新职业也就诞生了。

**A small example 一个例子**

Let me give you a real example from my own life.
我举一个我自己生活里的例子。

Last month I asked AI: "Write about climate change."
上个月我问 AI："写一篇关于气候变化的文章。"

I got a boring answer, like a bad textbook.
我得到一个很无聊的答案，像一本很差的教科书。

Then I changed my prompt. I said: "You are a science teacher. Explain climate change to a 12-year-old, in five short sentences, using one everyday example."
然后我改了 prompt。我说："你是一位科学老师，用五句短句向一个 12 岁孩子解释气候变化，并举一个日常例子。"

The second answer was clear, friendly, and useful.
第二个答案清楚、友好、有用。

Same AI. Same day. Different question. That is the whole idea of this job.
同一个 AI，同一天，不同的问题。这就是这个职业的全部核心。

**Transition 过渡**

So the job was born from a gap. But how did it grow so fast, and where is it now?
所以这个职业诞生于一个缺口。但它怎么长这么快？现在又在哪里？

My teammate will tell you the history. Thank you.
我的队友会来讲它的发展历程。谢谢。

> **背诵提示 Memorizing tips:**
> - Memorize the structure, not the words: **Hello → 2 questions → gap → prompt → 2022 → my example**.
>   记结构不记词：**开场 → 两个提问 → 缺口 → prompt → 2022 → 我的例子**。
> - The climate change example is your safety net. If you forget everything else, tell that story.
>   气候变化这个例子是你的安全网。就算别的都忘了，把这个故事讲出来。
> - When you ask the two questions, really wait and look at the audience. Silence is powerful.
>   问那两个问题时，真的停顿，看着观众。沉默很有力量。

## Script 2 — Member 2: Development History 职业发展历程

**Speaker 组员 2｜Time 时长: 4–5 min｜PPT: Slide 4**

---

**Opening 开场**

Thank you. I will talk about the history of this job.
谢谢。我来讲这个职业的发展历程。

It is a very short history — only about five years. But it is like a roller coaster.
这段历史很短，只有大约五年，但像坐过山车一样。

**Stage 1: The explorers 阶段一：探索者（2020–2022）**

Before the job had a name, some people were already doing it.
在这个职业有名字之前，已经有人在做了。

They shared their prompts online for free. They were just curious people who loved playing with AI.
他们在网上免费分享自己的 prompt。他们只是喜欢玩 AI 的好奇的人。

One famous person from that time is Riley Goodside. He posted his experiments with GPT-3 on Twitter.
那个时期有个名人叫 Riley Goodside，他在推特上发布自己用 GPT-3 做的实验。

He invented some methods that we still use today. For example, "Chain of Thought" — that means asking AI to think step by step.
他发明了一些我们今天仍在用的方法。比如 "Chain of Thought"（思维链）——就是让 AI 一步一步思考。

And "Role Play" — telling AI "you are a teacher" or "you are a doctor".
还有 "Role Play"（角色扮演）——告诉 AI"你是老师"或"你是医生"。

So the skills came first. The job title came later.
所以是先有技能，后有岗位名称。

**Stage 2: The gold rush 阶段二：淘金热（2023）**

Then, in November 2022, ChatGPT changed everything.
然后，2022 年 11 月，ChatGPT 改变了一切。

Millions of people started using it in just a few months.
短短几个月，数百万人开始使用它。

Companies suddenly needed people who could get the best results from AI.
公司突然需要能让 AI 产出最佳结果的人。

So in 2023, the job exploded. It became famous as "the sexiest job of the year".
于是 2023 年，这个岗位爆发了，被称为"年度最性感职业"。

The salary was amazing. One company, Anthropic, offered up to 335,000 US dollars a year for a prompt engineer.
薪水惊人。有一家公司 Anthropic，为提示词工程师开出最高年薪 33.5 万美元。

And here is the surprising part. That job did not require a computer science degree.
而且令人意外的是：这个岗位不要求计算机学位。

A survey found that nearly 29 percent of companies planned to hire prompt engineers that year.
一项调查发现，那一年近 29% 的公司计划招聘提示词工程师。

People said you could earn a high salary just by chatting with AI.
人们说，只要会跟 AI 聊天，就能拿高薪。

**Stage 3: The reality check 阶段三：现实检验（2024–2025）**

But the good times did not last long. And the reason is actually good news.
但好日子没持续太久。而原因其实是好消息。

The AI models got much smarter. GPT-4 and later models needed less hand-holding.
AI 模型变聪明多了。GPT-4 及之后的模型不再需要那么多"手把手教"。

When the model understands you better, writing prompts becomes easier. So writing prompts alone is no longer enough.
当模型更懂你，写 prompt 就变简单了。所以只会写 prompt 已经不够了。

In 2025, an OpenAI researcher named Sean Grove said something shocking. He said, "Prompt engineering is dead."

第二步：做设计。提示词工程师会问："用户是谁？AI 绝对不能说哪些话？我们怎么衡量成功？"

第四步：测试。再测，再测。

有一点大多数人不知道：测试是这个工作最耗时的部分。

一个项目可能需要上百次测试。有时他们要写个小程序来自动跑完所有测试。

第五步：建立评分标准。怎么知道 prompt 够好了？他们定规则，给大量答案打分。

第六步：交付并解释。他们必须清楚告诉团队，为什么这个 prompt 有效。

所以你看，写只是六步里的一步。

提示词工程师不会只说"请总结一下"。那太简单了。

最后那点非常重要。"不要添加文件中没有的内容"——这一句就防止了 AI 编造内容。

现在市场的要求是：别只写一个好的 prompt，要搭一整套工作流，把多个 AI 工具连起来。

所以工作是六步，领域正走向专家化和搭建者。

但这是好职业吗？我的队友会给你们客观的数字。谢谢。

对我们来说好消息是：你自己的专业也可以是优势——医学、法律、商科、设计，任何专业。

But I want to be honest about one important detail. A degree helps you get an interview.
但有一个重要细节我要说实话：学历帮你拿到面试。

It does not get you the job. In this field, they test your real skill.
但拿不到工作。这个领域考的是你的真实技能。

So think of the degree as a ticket. The skill is what you do inside the door.
把学历当成门票。技能才是进门之后要做的事。

**Hard skills 硬技能**

Now, hard skills. I have seven, but I will focus on the most important ones.
现在讲硬技能。我有七项，但重点讲最重要的几项。

Number one: clear writing. This is more important than programming.
第一：清晰的写作能力。这比编程更重要。

If you cannot explain what you want clearly, AI cannot help you.
如果你说不清自己想要什么，AI 就帮不了你。

Number two: logical thinking. Good prompts follow a clear order, step by step.
第二：逻辑思维。好的 prompt 有清晰的顺序，一步一步来。

Number three: basic programming. Not a computer science degree — just basic Python and how to use an API.
第三：基础编程。不需要计算机学位，只要基础 Python 和会用 API。

Number four: skill with AI tools. You should know the main tools well, including their limits.
第四：熟练使用 AI 工具。你要熟悉主流工具，包括它们的局限。

Number five: testing and evaluation. Remember, testing is the most time-consuming part of the job.
第五：测试与评估能力。记住，测试是这个工作最耗时的部分。

You have to check hundreds of answers and judge them fairly.
你要检查上百个答案，并公平地评判它们。

Number six: data analysis. Basic Excel and SQL are usually enough to start.
第六：数据分析。基础 Excel 和 SQL 通常就够入门了。

Number seven, and the most valuable: domain knowledge. I will say it again — this is the one that pays.
第七，也是最值钱的：行业知识。我再说一遍——这才是给你高薪的那一项。

**Soft skills 软技能**

Finally, soft skills. Do not skip this part. In this job, soft skills are half the job.
最后是软技能。别跳过这部分。在这个岗位上，软技能占了一半。

First: communication. You are a bridge. You translate between people and AI.
第一：沟通能力。你是一座桥，在人和 AI 之间做翻译。

You must understand a product manager, and then explain it to the AI in clear words.
你要听懂产品经理，再用清晰的话讲给 AI。

Second: curiosity. Technology changes every few months. Nobody can teach you everything.
第二：好奇心。技术每几个月就变一次，没人能教完你所有东西。

If you are curious, you will teach yourself. That is the real requirement.
如果你好奇，你会自学。这才是真正的要求。

Third: patience. Remember the hundreds of tests? Impatient people quit early.
第三：耐心。还记得上百次测试吗？没耐心的人会早早放弃。

Fourth: critical thinking. This one is very important. AI can be wrong, and it can sound very confident while being wrong.
第四：批判性思维。这点非常重要。AI 会出错，而且错的时候听起来还很自信。

Your job is to check it, not to trust it.
你的工作是检查它，而不是相信它。

Fifth: teamwork. You will work with product managers, designers, and programmers every day.
第五：团队协作。你每天要跟产品经理、设计师、程序员一起工作。

Sixth: never-stop learning. This field does not allow you to stop.
第六：终身学习。这个领域不允许你停下来。

**Closing 结语**

I want to end with one sentence that our group really believes.
我想用一句我们小组真心相信的话结尾。

Hard skills get you an interview. Soft skills get you the job.
硬技能给你面试，软技能给你工作。

And for an emerging profession, the ability to keep learning is the most important skill of all.
而对一个新兴职业来说，持续学习的能力才是最重要的技能。

**Handover 交接**

That is the end of our six parts. My teammate will now give a short summary.
我们六个部分到这里结束。我的队友现在做一个简短总结。

Thank you very much.
非常感谢。

> **背诵提示 Memorizing tips:**
> - Three parts: **Education → Hard Skills (7) → Soft Skills (6)**. Tell the audience the count before you start each part — it helps them follow.
>   三部分：**学历 → 硬技能（7 项）→ 软技能（6 项）**。每部分开头先报数量，观众更容易跟上。
> - If you run out of time, cut hard skills to the top three: **writing, logic, domain knowledge**.
>   如果时间不够，硬技能只讲前三：**写作、逻辑、行业知识**。
> - Your final line is the group's slogan. Say it slowly and clearly.
>   你最后那句是全组的口号，说慢一点、说清楚。

## Step 5: What the evaluation form likely rewards 步骤 5：评分表可能看重的点

Your teacher mentions an evaluation form on Page 21. Most forms like this score the same things. Aim at these:
老师提到第 21 页的评价表。这类表通常评同样的东西，朝这些方向准备：
